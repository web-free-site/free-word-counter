How to count characters for a meta title

Search result titles and meta descriptions are often shortened in the results page. A character count will not tell you the exact pixel width Google will show, but it is a practical way to keep titles and descriptions tight before you publish.

Steps

  1. Paste only the title or description. Open Free Word Counter and paste the proposed title tag or meta description on its own, not the whole page.
  2. Use the character count, not just words. Titles and descriptions are limited by space, so watch the character total (and characters without spaces if you need that view).
  3. Edit for sense, then recount. Shorten vague phrases first. Keep the main keyword in a natural sentence. Recount after every edit.
  4. Check the live preview later. After you publish, look at the snippet in a search preview or in the search results. Pixel width and device size still affect what gets cut off.

A practical writing range

A common working range is to keep titles concise (often in the neighborhood of 50-60 characters) and descriptions a little longer, then verify in a preview. These are writing guides, not guarantees, because search engines may rewrite snippets.

Other short-text uses

The same character count is useful for app store subtitles, short notices, and form fields that enforce a maximum length. Always confirm the current limit in the product you are writing for.
